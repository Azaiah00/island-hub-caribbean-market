How it works

Four steps from Midlothian Tpke to yard.

Pack it right, and we take it from there.

  1. 1

    Ask for a quote

    Call or text (804) 716-5480, message @islandhub_ on Instagram, or send the form below. Tell us what you're shipping and where.

  2. 2

    Get your barrel

    Need a barrel? Buy one in store at 6856 Midlothian Turnpike, Suite 104.

  3. 3

    Pack it right

    Fill your barrel, box or crate. Not sure if something can go? Ask us before you pack.

  4. 4

    Drop off or pickup

    Ask about drop-off at the store, or book a pickup in and around RVA (fees apply). Then we ship it.

Shipping questions

Before you pack.

Which islands do you ship to?

We ship to every Caribbean island. If you are not sure about your destination, call or text (804) 716-5480 and ask.

What can I ship in a barrel or box?

Barrels, boxes and crates can carry household goods, appliances, groceries and general items. Ask us before you pack if you are unsure about an item.

Do you sell shipping barrels?

Yes. Barrels are sold in store at 6856 Midlothian Turnpike, Suite 104.

Can you pick up my barrel?

Yes. We pick up barrels in and around Richmond, VA. Pickup fees apply, so ask for the price when you request your quote.

How much does it cost to ship a barrel?

Rates are quoted per shipment. Call or message us, or use the quote form on this page, and we will get back to you with a price.

Do you ship groceries within the US?

Yes, to all states. Perishable items ship overnight.
