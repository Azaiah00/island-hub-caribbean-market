Questions

Asked and answered.

What are Island Hub Caribbean Market's hours?

Monday to Wednesday 9am to 8pm, Thursday to Saturday 9am to 9pm, and Sunday 9am to 5pm. We are open seven days a week.

Where is Island Hub Caribbean Market?

6856 Midlothian Turnpike, Suite 104, Richmond, VA 23225, on Midlothian Turnpike in Southside Richmond.

Do you only carry Jamaican food?

No. We are Jamaican at heart, but the shelves cover the whole Caribbean, including Trinidadian drinks, Guyanese chow mein, Haitian favorites, African goods and more.

Do you ship barrels to the Caribbean?

Yes. We ship barrels, boxes and crates to every Caribbean island, including barrels, boxes, appliances, groceries and general goods. We also sell barrels and offer barrel pickup in and around RVA (fees apply).

How much does shipping cost?

Every shipment is quoted individually. Call or text (804) 716-5480, message @islandhub_ on Instagram, or send the shipping quote form on our Shipping page.

Can you ship Caribbean groceries to another state?

Yes. We ship grocery orders to all US states. Perishable items ship overnight.

Can I order online?

There is no online checkout. Call or text (804) 716-5480, send a direct message on Instagram, or use the request form, and we will follow up with you.
