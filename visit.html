The family

Family-run from day one.

Jamaican at heart, the whole Caribbean on the shelves.

Island Hub Caribbean Market opened on Midlothian Turnpike in May 2023, founded by Carlton Chambers and his daughter Tracy. The flags strung along the wall say it plainly: this store is for the whole Caribbean, plus neighbors who just want a good patty.

Come for one soda, leave with bush tea, a Ludi board and a barrel booked for home.

Visiting questions

Good to know.

Do I need an appointment to shop?

No. Just walk in any day during store hours.

What are your hours today?

Monday to Wednesday 9am to 8pm, Thursday to Saturday 9am to 9pm, and Sunday 9am to 5pm.

How do I know what is in stock?

Stock changes often. Follow @islandhub_ on Instagram for restocks, or call (804) 716-5480 before you come.

Can you get an item you do not carry?

Send us a request with the form on this page, or call, text or message us on Instagram. Tell us the brand and size if you know it.

Who owns Island Hub Caribbean Market?

Island Hub is family-run. It opened in May 2023, founded by Carlton Chambers and his daughter Tracy.

Do you have fresh produce?

Yes, when it is in: callaloo, breadfruit, yellow yam and seasonal sorrel around Christmas. Produce comes and goes, so check Instagram or call first.
